I am a Ph.D. student at the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Se-Young Yun.

My research focuses on fundamental algorithms and architectures for generative models , particularly diffusion models, efficient inference, and adaptive computation .

I am currently interested in recursive and looped architectures, efficient generative modeling, and multimodal/world models.

Research

My research aims to develop new algorithms and architectures for generative models, with an emphasis on improving generation, inference, and computation.

Generative Modeling

Diffusion models, discrete diffusion, flow-based generation, and one-step generative modeling.

Efficient Inference

Fast diffusion sampling, speculative decoding, and efficient generative inference.

Adaptive Architectures

Recursive and looped Transformers, adaptive computation, and hybrid architectures.

AI for Science

Molecular generation, protein design, scientific generative modeling, and weather forecasting.
