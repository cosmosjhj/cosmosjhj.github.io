I am a Ph.D. student at the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Se-Young Yun.

My research focuses on fundamental algorithms and architectures for generative models, with particular interests in efficient generation, adaptive computation, and model architectures.

Recently, I have been particularly interested in recursive and looped architectures and their applications to multimodal and generative models.

Research

I am interested in developing new algorithms and architectures that improve how generative models generate, reason, and allocate computation. My current research spans the following directions.

Generative Modeling

Diffusion models, discrete diffusion, flow-based generation, and efficient generative algorithms.

Efficient Inference

Fast sampling, speculative decoding, and efficient inference for modern generative models.

Recursive & Adaptive Architectures

Looped Transformers, parameter reuse, adaptive computation, and hybrid model architectures.

World Models & AI for Science

Generative modeling for dynamic and scientific systems, including multimodal data, molecules, proteins, and weather.
